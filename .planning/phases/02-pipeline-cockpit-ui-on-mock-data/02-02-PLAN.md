<objective>
Expand the proven tracer into the full pipeline board and a working sim clock. After this plan someone can drive the clock through the week and watch deals arrive, move, freeze when held and turn amber when stuck, on a board that follows the Playbook recipe and the Institutional tokens. Success criteria 1, 2 and 5 of the phase are met here for the board and clock; plans 03 and 04 add the deal detail, inbox and alerts.

Purpose: UI-02 needs SLA timers and needed-by badges on every row, UI-03 needs the clock, and UI-01 needs formatting to be one disciplined module. An automated audit script is added now so every later plan can prove red, amber, serif and the forbidden-style rules mechanically instead of by eye.

Output: finished formatting module, three extra pure time functions with tests, shared display primitives, the board with full row fidelity, the clock bar and store, client tests that pin the request shapes, and the audit gate.

## Decision coverage in this plan
D-01 (states on every board and clock path), D-02, D-05 (red only for holds, amber for SLA above 100 percent and at-risk or overdue deadlines), D-06 (money right-aligned, tabular), D-07, D-08 to D-12 (enforced by the audit), D-13, D-15 (legal name plus folder), D-16 (sober held and watch badges), D-18 (board derived from timestamps; needed-by badge labelled as of end of run), D-20 (neutral errors, inline confirmations only), D-21 (clock in client state, advance by hours or to a time, replay).

## Playbook deviation (flagged)
UI_PLAYBOOK section 4 says at most one badge per card. This plan applies the Held over Watch priority to one status slot, and keeps the needed-by badge as its own element, because ROADMAP Phase 2 success criterion 1 requires a needed-by badge wherever a deal has one. At most two badges can appear.

## Flagged assumptions (spec-less edge fallback)
- UI-03 (unclassified): bounds are the clock's start and end; replay returns to start and then plays at 1 sim hour per real second with Pause and Resume; the mock clock is stateless so the client clock is authoritative; a live engine clock is Phase 7's concern.
- UI-02 (unclassified): a stage's time limit is shown only when at least one deal in that column provides sla_hours; the board never infers limits from the rulebook.
- The end of a stage entry ends its SLA clock (a held, declined or closed deal stops accruing). This is a derived rule not shown by any fixture beyond the held deals; confirm against the live engine at the Phase 7 switch.
- The needed-by badge and watch flag carry no timestamp, so they show the end-of-run value even when the clock is earlier (D-18).
