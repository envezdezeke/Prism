<objective>
Let the user open any deal and see what the engine read and why it decided what it did. This plan adds the left rail and navigation, the hash-addressable deal route, the plain-words label and people maps, and the full deal detail screen. It delivers success criterion 3 of the phase (UI-04) except for inbox-answer overlays, which plan 04 layers on the requests section.

Purpose: the demo's trust moment is opening a deal and seeing facts with provenance, issues in plain words, the stage log's reasons, and the fraud panel for a held deal. D-14 and D-17 mean the people shown must be names or role labels, never ids; D-18 means facts and issues are honestly labelled as end-of-run values while stage log, requests and alerts follow the clock.

Output: label and people modules with tests, route helpers with tests, three small time functions with tests, the UI store, the app frame with a one-entry rail, board rows that open the detail, and the detail screen.

## Decision coverage in this plan
D-01 (states, no invented fields), D-02 (no router library: a zustand view plus hash), D-05 (green only for cleared or approved answers, amber for escalations and deadlines), D-11 (rail lists only built views; real banking vocabulary), D-14 and D-17 (names from a hand-written map, role labels otherwise, id in tooltip), D-15 (legal name plus folder; DBA omitted because the contract has no field), D-16 (sober fraud panel), D-18 (as-of-end-of-run captions on facts and issues; derived stage log, requests and alerts), D-20 (neutral errors, no toasts).

## Playbook items consciously not built (no contract field, or Phase 7, or stretch)
Source-document side sheet (the contract carries no document snippet; the chip is plain text), the F1 to F6 bits-versus-threshold list in the fraud panel (graph evidence is Phase 7, UI-06), the side-by-side mismatch view and keyboard shortcuts (optional stretch, section 5 of the Playbook). The Playbook's Credit officer summary row is shown only when the deal has a credit_decision request to read it from.

## Flagged assumptions (spec-less edge fallback)
- UI-04 (concurrency, resolved as a truth above): derived views are pure functions of (data, now), so interruption or rapid clock changes never tear the screen; a failed detail fetch is isolated to its own deal.
- The directory names map is hand-written from the bank's published staff list (names only). If the live engine uses directory ids, they will resolve to names automatically; the five fixture ids that are not in the directory (credit.nakamura, committee.osei, fraudops.keller, mgr.ibarra, rm.alvarez) show role labels per D-17, and the fixtures are not changed.
- Alert recipients have no request type, so the role label comes from the alert's level and stage (stage owner role for an alert, Escalation contact for an escalation).
