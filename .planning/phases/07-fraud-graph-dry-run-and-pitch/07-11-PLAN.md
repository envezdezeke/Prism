<objective>
Mirror the cluster's signal-family checklist on the deal page and add a one-click link into the fraud graph at that deal's cluster.

Purpose: Phase 2's UI-SPEC deferred the F1 to F6 evidence on the deal page to Phase 7 (row 12 of its resolved conflicts). With the checklist built on the graph page in 07-03, a held deal can show why it was held where a credit officer expects to look, and the demo can go from the deal to the graph in one click. This is the first thing to cut if time is short: it is extra deal-detail polish, and the graph page already carries the same evidence.

Output: an optional cluster id in the graph route, the mirror and link on the deal page, and the graph page selecting the cluster from the route.
