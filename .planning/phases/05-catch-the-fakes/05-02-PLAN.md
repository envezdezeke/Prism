<objective>
Make the two built signal families (F3 lookalike vendor, F5 deposit pattern) and the threshold calibration trustworthy on a history the team has never seen. Today the calibration is wired to the sample's dates (a differently dated history silently turns F3 and F5 off, which means no hold at all), F3 fires on 36% of honest equipment loans because vendors carry a different last four on every deal, and F5 fires on any weekday label the 104-deal reference has not seen.

Purpose: red-team R4 (critical for the hidden set), R2 and R8. These are the inputs the hold rule counts, so they have to be specific before more families are added.
Output: calibrate.py without date literals, F3 and F5 fixes in signals.py, a rank-based measurement split, new tests, and before and after bench numbers.

This plan edits no config file: its knobs are documented module constants next to the code, the same way `rarity.PRIOR_STRENGTH` already is.
</objective>
