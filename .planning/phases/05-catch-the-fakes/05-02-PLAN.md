<objective>
Finish the scored fraud outputs and the generalization layer: linked deals and history flags from an explicit independent-link rule, the watch tier with per-family evidence, the trap regression, and the remaining signal families F2, F4 and F6.

Purpose: after Plan 01 the tuning line is route 40/40, stage 10/10, flag 10/10, held 10/10, but links stay 8/10 (the ring deals list only current partners) and `history_flags` is empty. The answer key wants each ring deal linked to the other ring deals and to exactly two history deals. A diagnostic run on the real history shows the rule works generically: the two ring history deals share many independent classes with a ring deal (preparer, phone, registered agent, remit account, vendor, producer, plus the same F3 and F5 pattern), while each decoy history deal shares exactly one (a lone producer or a lone registered agent). FRD-03 and the core value also need families that do not depend on one trick: with only F3 and F5 as voting extras, a hidden-set ring that lacks a lookalike vendor or a deposit pattern would never reach F1 plus two.

FRD-01 (history baseline) and FRD-02 (entity graph with rarity weights) were built by quick task 261107-qx4. This plan is where they are exercised by the hold path and verified end to end, and where the baseline gains the terms distribution that F6 needs.

Output: links and cluster module; filled linked_deals, history_flags and clusters; trap regression and history hold-rule report; F2, F4, F6; final gate with the one-time holdout aggregate.

Order inside this plan (do not reorder): Task 1 links and history flags (the scored part), Task 2 regression and the history report, Task 3 families F2/F4/F6. Task 3 is the last decision-affecting change and the cut boundary: if the orchestrator stops the plan after Task 2, FRD-03 is recorded as partial in the SUMMARY (F2, F4, F6 reported "not evaluated" in clusters) and nothing else is lost.

Boundaries (unchanged from Plan 01): do not edit prism/read/**, prism/move/**, prism/api/**, prism/engine/run.py or ui/. No sample literals and no history id pattern (an H- followed by four digits) in prism/ or config/fraud.yaml, including docstrings and comments. Holdout rules D-02/D-18: tuning deals only until the single `--final` gate at the end.
