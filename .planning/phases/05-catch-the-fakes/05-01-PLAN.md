<objective>
Make `decide()` produce holds. This is the tracer for the whole phase: one ring, injected at the feature layer (entities plus deposit rows, exactly what Read will leave behind), goes through `evaluate()` and comes out held at the right tick, with sticky blocks, linked deals, history flags and the OLD_TRICK path, while an honest look-alike stays unheld. Everything later (calibration, hygiene, F4, F2, graph view, trap harness) improves what this plan makes possible.

Purpose: without `decide()` the shipped run flags nothing (red-team R1). This is the minimum shippable win: holds, linked deals, history flags, zero false holds on the twins.
Output: `prism/fraud/cluster.py`, a real `prism/fraud/decide.py`, the `links` block in `config/fraud.yaml`, the synth harness writing blocks back like `run.py`, and `tests/test_fraud_decide.py`.

Why `prism/engine/run.py` is not touched: `run.py` overwrites `states[name].fraud` with `outcome.per_deal[name]` and calls `evaluate` inside one try block. Stickiness is handled by `decide` returning every previously held block again, and a crash inside one component is caught inside `decide`, so run.py needs no change (red-team R10).
</objective>
