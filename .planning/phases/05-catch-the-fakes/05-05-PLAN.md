Output: forensics.py, terms.py, `FamilySignal.basis`, the F4 threshold in calibration, the basis rule in decide, and tests.

Read is still a stub. F4a runs on the `pdf_producer` entity (which Read and the bench both supply). F4b reads `Doc.doc_class` and `Doc.meta["producer"]` behind the Read interface: it returns nothing while those are absent and starts working the moment Read fills them.
