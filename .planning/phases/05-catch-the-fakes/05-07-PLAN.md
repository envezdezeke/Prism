  <behavior>
    - `poisson_tail_bits(m, lam)` = `-log2(P(X >= m))` for X Poisson with mean `lam`, computed as `1 - sum_{i<m} e^-lam lam^i / i!`, with the tail clamped to at least 1e-300 and the result 0.0 when the tail is 1 or m is 0.
    - Long-run rate: `span = max(1, last_applied - first_applied)` days over the whole history; for an entity used by `df` history deals (leave-one-out where relevant) `lam = (df + 1) * window_days / span`.
    - `score_f2(folder, my_date, profile, current_dates, baseline, params, weak_kinds, window_days)`: for each (kind, value) in the profile whose kind is not weak and whose history frequency is rare (`df / n < params.rho`; an unseen value counts as rare), `m` = number of other current deals carrying the value with a date within `window_days` plus history deals carrying it with `applied` within `window_days`; entities with `m == 0` are skipped; bits per entity = `poisson_tail_bits(m, lam)`; the deal's bits are the maximum; evidence "a rare <kind label> was used by <m> other deals within <window_days> days; its usual rate is about <lam:.2f> in such a window" (no value in the text). Returns None without a date or without a qualifying entity.
    - Calibration: `tau["F2"]` = max(95th percentile of the leave-one-out F2 bits over the clean set, `floor_bits(q)`); for a clean history deal h the window is centered on h's date, "other deals" are history deals other than h, and `df` excludes h. `fire_rate_clean["F2"]` reported.
    - Cases (fake history of 150 deals spread over 300 days, entities unseen unless stated): two current deals sharing an unseen preparer 3 days apart fire F2 (bits above tau); the same two 200 days apart do not (m = 0); a preparer used by 40 history deals never counts (not rare); address and bank never count; three deals sharing an unseen agent within 10 days give higher bits than two; the family is absent without dates; leave-one-out calibration on a history with no bursts gives tau equal to the floor.
    - `compute_signals` emits F2 with `basis = ()`, ordered F1, F2, F3, F4, F5 per deal.
  </behavior>
